Gamification Rules · 20 August 2026

Two systems sit on a participant's account. They are kept separate on purpose: Trust Score measures reliability, while Points are rewards that can be redeemed.

5
GuardrailsRules that keep the system fair
  • No public leaderboard. A ranking would make a professional panel feel like a game.
  • Points can never raise or lower the Trust Score, in either direction.
  • Each study can set its own rule on repeat participants: allow them, prefer fresh people, or exclude anyone who has taken part before.
6
The one number we need from youEverything else is set
What a point is worth in money. Our proposal is 100 points equals one dollar, so a completed study earns about 25 cents on top of the incentive, and a referral who reaches Gold is worth around two dollars fifty.
  • Set it lower and the rewards stop motivating anyone. Set it higher and the referral bonuses start competing with the incentives themselves.
  • Client ratings are confirmed as part of this build, so that question is closed. They are the three states already in the workflow: poor, good or excellent.
