Trust and Rewards Policy

Focus Insite · Version 2 · 22 October 2026

This policy governs participant Trust Scores, tiers, reward points, redemption, scheduling, restriction, client ratings, the certificate and client fees. Where any design, screen or document states a different figure, this policy is the one to follow. It is kept current with the decisions made as the platform is built, and the platform must never disagree with it.

It replaces the version of 13 October 2026, which stays on file unchanged. Rows shaded yellow are changes from that version. Rows shaded red are open decisions. Everything else is unchanged.

1. Trust Score

A participant's reputation, earned by behaviour. It is never set by hand for one participant.

A participant starts at 50. Completing onboarding is what earns that 50: it is the starting score, not something added on top of it. Everything else in this table is added to or taken from that 50.

The score is the starting 50 plus everything earned and deducted since, kept within 50 and 100. A deduction that would take the score below 50 still counts: the score shows 50, and the deduction comes off later gains before the score rises again. For example, a participant at 50 who cancels a session (−2) stays at 50, and their next completed study (+1) leaves them at 50, not 51.

A no show costs more than a cancellation on purpose. Cancelling properly costs 2. If not turning up also cost 2, there would be no reason to cancel rather than simply not appear, and the client would lose the slot without warning. One design screen shows No show at −2; this policy is the one to follow.

Adding a profile picture or an intro video earns reward points but does not affect the Trust Score. The Trust Score is earned by behaviour in studies, not by filling in a profile. Adding a professional credential does not change the Trust Score either.

2. Tiers

TierTrust Score
Silver50 and above

Tier is derived from the Trust Score. It is never set independently and never stored as a fact of its own.

Under Gold and Platinum the app shows "In Top N%": the share of active, ID-verified participants at that tier or above, worked out from the platform at the time it is shown. Silver has no such line: it is where everyone starts, so a percentile there means nothing.

The profile picture and the intro video each earn their points once. Removing either afterwards does not take the points back.

A participant who completes their profile, adds a picture and adds a video earns 150 points in total, which is $1.50.

Consent

A profile picture and an intro video each carry their own consent, which is off by default.

  • Points are earned for adding the picture or video, whether or not consent is given.
  • A client sees the picture only if consent on the picture is on, and the video only if consent on the video is on.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time, and the client stops seeing it immediately.
  • With consent off, a client sees the participant's initial.

A completed study's reward is paid into the participant's wallet by the team, by hand, within 14 days after the study ends. A withdrawal from the wallet then reaches the bank in 2 to 3 working days. The two are separate steps. The team's console shows, for every study waiting to be paid, the days left of the 14 counted from when the study ended, and marks any past them as overdue.

The rate card

Since 24 October 2026 the price comes from the team's rate card (the July 2026 sheet). It is confidential: no client screen shows it. The team sees the breakdown; the client sees one cost per interview times the number of participants; the participant sees only their incentive.

A restricted participant cannot take part in studies, withdraw earnings or redeem rewards for the period. They are told that they are restricted, which category it was, and when it ends.

A deactivated participant is sent the written reason and can appeal.

10. Changing these values

RuleValue new
What can be changedTrust Score values (section 1), reward point values (section 3), tier thresholds (section 2), the team's fee cards (section 8), and since 23 October 2026 the participant fees: the withdrawal fee and the value of 100 reward points (section 4). A study's cost per interview is set per study (section 8)
Who can change themA master admin, from the console. Each change is confirmed first and recorded with who made it and when
What a change affectsOnly what happens after the change

A changed value applies only to what happens after the change. Anything already earned or already agreed keeps the value that was in force at the time. No existing score moves when a value is edited, and no agreed price moves: a study keeps the fees it was created with.

A withdrawal already requested keeps the fee in force when it was requested, and points already redeemed keep the value they were redeemed at. The minimum redemption (section 4), scheduling (section 5), client ratings (section 6), the certificate (section 7) and restriction (section 9) are not changed from the console. They change only by a new version of this policy.
